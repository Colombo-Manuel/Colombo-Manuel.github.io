EA-IRMS

Our Elemental Analyzer-IRMS (Costech ECS 4010 CHNS-O & DeltaV IRMS) is optimized for stable carbon and nitrogen isotopic analyses (δ¹³C and δ¹⁵N) in solid matrices. We also perform %OC and %N analysis. Services include C & N in solids, tinning, acid fumigation, and difficult sample processing.
