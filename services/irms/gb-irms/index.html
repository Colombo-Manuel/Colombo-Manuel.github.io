GB-IRMS

Our Gas Bench-IRMS (Delta-V advantage IRMS with dual inlet and Conflo IV interfaced with a GasBench II peripheral, Thermo Fisher Scientific) is optimized for continuous flow isotope ratio determinations of CO₂ and N₂ headspace gases (δ¹³C and δ¹⁵N).
