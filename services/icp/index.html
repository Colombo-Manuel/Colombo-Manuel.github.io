VIMS TQ-ICP-MS Lab

Our Lab houses an inductively coupled plasma triple-quadrupole mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with an automated high-throughput sample introduction system (ESI 2DX FAST) enclosed within a polypropylene laminar-flow hood for minor and trace elemental analysis.
